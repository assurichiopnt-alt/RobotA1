Why look at the iiMT CI series

  • Five sizes in one series (CI-03, CI-05, CI-10, CI-16, CI-20): one supplier and one training path for several tasks.
  • Repeatability ±0.02 mm (CI-03, CI-05) and ±0.05 mm (CI-10, CI-16, CI-20) per the iiMT datasheet.
  • CE and CNAS certified, IP54, collision detection and drag teaching on every size.
  • Programming from graphical pendant to Python, C++, Lua and ROS, with Modbus/TCP communication for existing machines.
  • Not sure which size you need? Send payload, reach and task: our engineers recommend a model and check integration.

Where it fits

Factories adding automation to an existing line: machine tending, handling, welding and similar tasks where a full fenced robot cell is not justified. iiMT cites 15+ industries and 200+ application scenarios. For portable MIG/MAG welding see the iiMT CI-05Wy.

Common to the series

  • 6 rotating joints, ±360° range, IP54, collision detection, drag teaching, safety-area setting.
  • I/O: 16 DI, 16 DO, 4 AI, 4 AO. Communication: TCP/IP, ModbusTCP, RS485.
  • Controller power 100–240 VAC, 50/60 Hz; arm supply 48 V. Ambient −10 to 60 °C.
  • CE and CNAS certified; 5 safety configurations (EN ISO 13849-1:2008, PLd). The catalogue states no safety fence is needed in suitable applications; we confirm this for your risk assessment.
  • Programming via graphical pendant, tablet, Lua, C++, Python, SDK and ROS. Typical deployment is quoted by iiMT at around 1 hour.

Source: iiMT product datasheets (CI series catalogue and welding catalogue). Values are as published by the manufacturer and may vary by configuration.

Questions

What models are in the CI series?

CI-03 (3 kg, 600 mm), CI-05 (5 kg, 900 mm), CI-10 (10 kg, 1,300 mm), CI-16 (16 kg, 1,500 mm) and CI-20 (20 kg, 1,650 mm). See the table above.

Which model should I choose?

Choose by payload (including the gripper), the reach you need and your task. Send us these and we recommend a model.

Does it need a safety fence?

The catalogue states no fence is needed in suitable applications, with 5 safety configurations (PLd). The final answer depends on your risk assessment; we help you check it.

Can it work with my existing machines?

Yes, it offers 16 DI/16 DO/4 AI/4 AO plus TCP/IP, ModbusTCP and RS485. We check the interface before quoting.
