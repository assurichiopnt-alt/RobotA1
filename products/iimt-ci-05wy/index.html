Why look at the iiMT CI-05Wy

  • Collaborative design with collision detection and safety-area setting; CE and CNAS certified platform (confirm the safety setup for your application).
  • Portable: magnetic base and mobile trolley, so you move it between workstations and fixture it where the work is.
  • MIG/MAG welding of carbon steel, stainless steel, copper and aluminium, 5–50 mm workable thickness.
  • Drag teaching with 4-step calibration and a welding process package with templates: shorter set-up for repeat seams.
  • Backed by Assurich Industries in Singapore: application review with our engineers, commissioning and after-sales support.

Where it fits

Fabrication shops and factories that weld small to medium parts, repeat the same seams often, or need one cobot to serve several stations. Sectors named by iiMT include marine, metal processing, rail, steel structure and automotive. For longer reach, see the iiMT-CW-05. For a general-purpose collaborative arm, see the iiMT CI series.

Platform and safety (CI cobot)

  • IP54 protection, 6 rotating joints with ±360° range, electromagnetic brakes.
  • Collision detection and safety-area setting; 5 safety configurations (EN ISO 13849-1:2008, PLd). CE and CNAS certified per the catalogue.
  • Drag teaching and a welding process package with templates. Programming via pendant, Lua, C++, Python, ROS2 and SDK.
  • Optional: laser seam tracking, arc tracking, molten-pool camera, teach-free 3D vision, expert library, 10.1-inch rugged pendant.

Questions

What are the key specifications?

5 kg payload and 900 mm reach on the CI-05 class arm. MIG/MAG process, 0.8–1.6 mm wire, 5–50 mm workable thickness, IP54. Repeatability is quoted as ±0.02 mm for the CI-W05/CI-05 in the iiMT datasheet; we confirm the figure for your exact configuration.

Can it be moved between workstations?

Yes. It is a portable package with a magnetic base and mobile trolley, and the catalogue notes it can be lifted with one hand. We confirm the fixturing and safety arrangement for your application.

Which welding machines does it work with?

iiMT lists several brands, including Panasonic, Aotai and Megmet, air or water cooled, pulse and non-pulse. Tell us your machine and wire and we confirm compatibility before quoting.

How is it programmed?

By drag teaching: you guide the arm along the seam and use the welding process package. A 4-step calibration sets the torch.
